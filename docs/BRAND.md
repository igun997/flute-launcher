# Flute brand

## Positioning

Flute started as a focus launcher. It is now **a lightweight launcher that boosts your productivity, and focus is only the start.**

- **One line:** Not only focus. Boost your productivity, in a lightweight launcher.
- **Three words:** Lightweight. Focused. Boosted.
- **For whom:** people who want a calmer phone that also gets things done, without another heavy app.
- **Promise:** it stays out of the way, and when you ask, it does the work.

| Pillar | Proof the app really has |
|---|---|
| Lightweight | one quiet Home, no widgets/feeds/ads/analytics; GPU scenes capped at 15 to 30 fps; Rust only where measured faster (docs/PERFORMANCE.md) |
| Focus | Free / Work / Focus / Sleep modes, schedules, pause gate, DND, screen time by category |
| Boost | Ask Flute: launcher control, calendar, notes, contacts, web search, images, Python sandbox with charts and files, routines, voice, MCP (GitHub, Jira, Simular Box) |
| Delight | weather sky from sunrise to storm, six chibi companions |

## Voice

Short, calm, confident. Verbs first. No hype words ("revolutionary", "supercharge"), no exclamation marks in the product,
one idea per line. In the PV the type is loud and the sentences stay short.

## Beat PV direction

A beat-synced promo: everything lands on the beat grid (120 BPM, one beat = 0.5 s, one bar = 2 s).

- Hard cuts and slams on the kick; on-the-"and" accents for small hits. No slow fades.
- Giant kinetic type, one to three words at a time, in flat colour blocks (ink, teal, cream, sun, coral), words stamped,
  stacked, sliced and echoed.
- Phones as props: real screenshots, tilted, punched in, split into strips, swapped on the beat.
- Flat bold illustration with thick shapes and a limited palette, never photoreal; characters pop in as stingers.
- White flash frames and shape wipes (circle, bar, diagonal) as the transitions.
- Safe areas respected; 9:16 first (1080 x 1920), with a 1:1 cut-down if wanted.
